Candles hand-poured and carved into roses, succulents and blooms. Perfumes hand-blended from real oudh, rose, tobacco and resin, then rested for weeks before bottling. Both made in small batches in Kolkata, carrying the small variations that come from not being mass-produced.

See the collections DM to order
"Every candle leaves the mould a little different, and every perfume changes slightly as it rests — that's not a flaw, that's the point."

Aerly began as an experiment in wax, colour and fragrance, and grew into a small Kolkata studio making candles shaped like roses, succulents, and festive blooms, alongside perfumes hand-blended and rested in small batches — each one finished by hand, never mass-produced.
